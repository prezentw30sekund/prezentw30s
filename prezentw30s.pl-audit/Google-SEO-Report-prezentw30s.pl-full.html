2. Audit Categories

2.1 Technical SEO

Score: 62/100

What Works

  • HTTPS, single-hop redirects
  • Absolute self canonicals
  • Static HTML

Findings

No robots.txt and sitemap High

Both 404.

Recommendation: public/robots.txt with Disallow: /go/ and Sitemap line; sitemap endpoint fed by one indexability helper.

Only HSTS among security headers High

No CSP, X-Content-Type-Options, Referrer-Policy, Permissions-Policy; ACAO * on HTML.

Recommendation: headers in vercel.json, CSP Report-Only first.

Duplicate URLs return 200 Medium

Slashless URLs and /index.html do not redirect.

Recommendation: trailingSlash true in vercel.json, redirect /index.html.

Default Vercel 404 Medium

Plain NOT_FOUND page.

Recommendation: src/pages/404.astro.

Manual noindex Medium

noindex not tied to product count.

Recommendation: Derive noindex from data.

2.2 Content Quality

Score: 36/100

What Works

  • Clear affiliate disclosure
  • Readable Polish, no AI boilerplate

Findings

No operator identity Critical

Kontakt and Polityka prywatności show a 'do uzupełnienia' placeholder.

Recommendation: Publish name, address, NIP from one source.

Visible placeholders High

[N] propozycji, zdjęcie, [DATA], [LISTA PROGRAMÓW], [OZNACZENIE].

Recommendation: Fill or hide; build check for bracket placeholders.

Budget sub-lists duplicate parent High

Same description, intro, FAQ; cumulative budgets repeat products.

Recommendation: Own copy per sub-list; noindex or canonical when identical.

Unkept price promise High

O nas promises price replacement after 30 days; not implemented.

Recommendation: Implement in ProductCard or soften.

Missing target URLs High

/mikolajki/do-50-zl/, /prezent/<slug>/, /go/ do not exist.

Recommendation: Decide routes before launch.
